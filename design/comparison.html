What changed in the blend

Slate Blue keeps
the quieter base.

Canvas, surfaces, borders, main/secondary text, and selected text come from Neutral Slate. Accent, focus, selection fill, and success/warning/error colors come from Graphite Blue.

The neutral login/lock colors, geometry, icons, cursor family, and Meslo terminal font are shared. Inter remains the UI proposal for Graphite Blue and Slate Blue; Neutral Slate originally used Noto Sans.

A useful distinction

Neutral Slate’s steel-blue accent is lighter, with higher luminance contrast against its surface. Graphite Blue and Slate Blue use a more saturated blue. Their extra “pop” comes from color character, rather than a higher contrast ratio.

“Warmer” here is relative: Neutral Slate and Slate Blue remain neutral-to-cool charcoals, with slightly warm off-white text. Neither has Ink Blue’s pronounced background cast.
