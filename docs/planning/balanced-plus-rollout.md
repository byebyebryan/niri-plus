# Crisper Neutral Slate and Balanced+ delivery

Accepted scope, 2026-10-26: select the crisper warm desktop and Balanced+ terminal
on fully opaque pure black. Maintain one identity with independent desktop and
terminal values, Noto Sans UI and Meslo terminal typography. Other comparisons
remain historical references.

The canonical identity moves to schema 2: `terminal.colors` owns framing and
`terminal.ansi` owns its sixteen entries. Desktop visited-link colors also have
their own role. The comparison uses an exact retained Clear Blue baseline, so
promoting the selection does not silently rewrite its earlier specimens.

## Delivery gates

1. Regenerate appearance examples from canonical chezmoi adapters. Validate
   source isolation/admission, native GTK3/GTK4/Qt6/Rofi/Kitty and derived greeter
   samples, and site build/browser behavior.
2. Publish the exact producer on a topic branch, verify its immutable archive
   and canonical identity bytes, then pin that tuple in chezmoi.
3. On Carbon, Starship and Snap, retain private preimages, review scoped
   status/diff/dry-run, apply only appearance targets and the pinned external,
   and reload by owner. Preserve unrelated source work and native sessions.
4. Verify exact installed values, scoped convergence and repeat application,
   record active versus next-login behavior, then publish site delivery data.

Recovery records were captured on all three hosts before appearance changes.
Carbon and Snap have active desktops. Starship has no graphical session;
install its files for the next login and preserve inactive desktop services.
No new desktop session, package/utility admission, device action, logout,
locker interruption or SDDM restart is part of this delivery.

Human visual/OLED checks, provider-owned RGB backgrounds and normal login/lock
flows remain distinct from source, installed-byte and reload verification.

## Delivery result

The immutable producer is `3fc6ba4b79631fbc4a7967830ca14bb84f1e9cbf`; applied
consumer is `b946b5e27936919d6277d7033a50d5e1d747abf9`. All three hosts have

reloaded in place; Starship retains inactive desktop services until a normal
graphical login. All 42 captured native processes, service/bus identities,
GSettings and unrelated source work were preserved. Each root greeter verifies
24 derived files with root ownership and mode 0644, without restarting SDDM.
